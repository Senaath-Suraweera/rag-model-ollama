# Driver Guide

## What does a RideMachan driver do?

Drivers drive customers in a rented vehicle for **with-driver** (chauffeur) bookings. A driver can be:

- **Independent** – registered directly with RideMachan and hired together with a vehicle from any provider who allows external drivers.
- **Company driver** – attached to a rental company and assigned to that company's vehicles.

## Requirements to become a driver

- Age 23 – 65.
- A valid Sri Lankan driving licence held for at least **3 years**, covering the vehicle classes you will drive (for vans, a light vehicle licence; for buses, a heavy vehicle licence).
- A police clearance report issued within the last 6 months.
- A medical fitness certificate from a registered doctor.
- A smartphone with the RideMachan Driver app installed and location services turned on.
- Ability to communicate in Sinhala or Tamil, and basic English. Additional languages (e.g., German, French, Chinese, Russian) can be added to the profile and help with tourist bookings.

## How do I register as a driver?

1. Sign up and choose **Driver**.
2. Verify your mobile number with OTP.
3. Upload your NIC, driving licence (front and back), police clearance report and medical certificate.
4. Add a clear profile photo, languages spoken and years of experience.
5. Add your bank account for payouts.
6. Choose **Independent** or request to join a rental company.

Verification takes 2–3 working days and includes a short phone interview.

## Joining a rental company

From **Rental Companies**, browse companies and send a join request. The company reviews your profile and accepts or rejects it. Once accepted, the company can assign vehicles and trips to you. You can see your assigned vehicle under **My Vehicle**. You can only belong to one rental company at a time.

## Accepting trips

- Independent drivers receive trip requests through notifications. Accept or decline within **30 minutes**.
- Company drivers receive trips assigned by their company and must confirm them in the app.
- Set your availability in **Availability** so you only get requests for days you can work.

## During a trip

1. Arrive at the pick-up location **at least 10 minutes early**.
2. Tap **Start Trip** when the customer is in the vehicle. Live location tracking starts automatically, and the customer and provider can see the vehicle's location.
3. Follow the itinerary agreed with the customer. Changes that add hours or kilometres must be confirmed by the customer in the app.
4. Record any fuel purchases in the app with a photo of the receipt if fuel is not included.
5. Tap **End Trip** at the end of each day (multi-day trips) and at the final drop-off.

## Working hours and rest

- The standard with-driver day is **8 hours and 100 km**. Extra hours are paid by the customer.
- Drivers must not drive more than **12 hours in a day**, including overtime.
- For multi-day trips outside your home district, the customer provides accommodation and meals, or pays a LKR 2,500 nightly allowance which is passed on to you in full.

## Driver code of conduct

Drivers must:
- Be polite, punctual and neatly dressed.
- Follow all traffic laws and speed limits. Traffic fines caused by the driver are the driver's responsibility.
- Never drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs. This results in immediate permanent removal.
- Never use a mobile phone while driving except hands-free for navigation.
- Not smoke in the vehicle.
- Not ask customers for cash payments, tips or extra charges outside the app. Customers may give tips voluntarily.
- Not carry other passengers or goods during a booking.

## Messages and notifications

Use **Messages** to talk with customers, providers and your rental company. Keep all communication about bookings inside the app so it can be reviewed if there is a dispute. Important updates (new trips, changes, cancellations, payouts) arrive in **Notifications**.

## How are drivers paid?

- Independent drivers receive the full driver fee and overtime minus a **10% RideMachan commission**, paid every Monday and Thursday for completed trips.
- Company drivers are paid by their rental company under their own agreement. RideMachan pays the company, not the driver.

## Driver ratings

Customers rate drivers from 1 to 5 stars. Drivers with an average below 4.0 over their last 20 trips receive a review and coaching call. An average below 3.5 may lead to suspension.
