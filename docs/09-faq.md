# Frequently Asked Questions

## Booking

**Q: Can I book a vehicle for today?**
A: Yes. Many Instant Book vehicles can be booked with as little as 2 hours' notice. Use the **Available now** filter. Request to Book vehicles need provider approval, which can take up to 4 hours.

**Q: Can I book without a credit card?**
A: Yes. You can pay with a debit card, mobile wallet (FriMi, Genie, eZ Cash) or bank transfer (for bookings at least 48 hours ahead). The security deposit for self-drive rentals needs a card or a wallet that supports holds.

**Q: Can I choose the exact car model?**
A: Yes. Each listing is a specific vehicle, so the car in the photos is the car you get. If the provider has to swap it, you must approve the replacement, or you can cancel for a full refund.

**Q: Can I pick up from one city and return in another (one-way rental)?**
A: Only if the provider offers one-way rentals. Look for the **One-way available** label. A one-way fee (usually LKR 5,000 – 15,000, depending on distance) is shown at checkout.

**Q: Do you deliver vehicles to hotels or the airport?**
A: Many providers offer delivery for a fee. Choose **Delivery** as the pick-up option and enter the address, or choose the airport. Airport delivery is available at Katunayake (CMB) and Mattala (HRI).

**Q: Can I rent a vehicle with a driver for a round-island tour?**
A: Yes. Choose **With driver**, set your full trip dates, and describe your itinerary in the booking note. Accommodation and meals for the driver are arranged by you, or a LKR 2,500 nightly allowance is added.

## Licences and documents

**Q: I'm a tourist. Can I drive in Sri Lanka with my home licence?**
A: Not by itself. You need a Sri Lankan temporary driving permit (from the DMT or the Automobile Association of Ceylon) or an International Driving Permit endorsed for Sri Lanka, together with your home licence. If you don't have one, book a vehicle **with a driver** instead.

**Q: My licence is a digital/temporary licence. Is that accepted?**
A: A temporary licence receipt from the DMT is accepted if it is still valid and shows the correct vehicle class. Upload it with your NIC.

**Q: Can I use a learner's permit?**
A: No. Learner's permits are not accepted for self-drive rentals.

## Payments and deposits

**Q: When will I get my deposit back?**
A: RideMachan releases it within 3 working days after a problem-free return. Your bank may take another 5–10 working days to show it.

**Q: Why was I charged after I returned the car?**
A: Extra charges can be for fuel, extra kilometres, late return, cleaning, tolls, fines or damage. You will receive an itemised receipt. If you disagree, open a dispute within 7 days.

**Q: Are highway tolls included?**
A: No. Expressway tolls (e.g., Southern Expressway, Katunayake Expressway, Outer Circular Highway) are paid by the customer. For with-driver bookings, the driver pays and the amount is added to your final bill with receipts.

**Q: Can I pay the driver or owner in cash?**
A: No. All payments must go through RideMachan. Payments outside the app are not protected by insurance, refunds or support.

## During the rental

**Q: What if I get stuck in traffic and will be late returning?**
A: Request an extension in the app as soon as you know. Returns up to 59 minutes late are free. After that, late return charges apply.

**Q: Can I take the car to Jaffna or the hill country?**
A: Yes, you can drive anywhere in Sri Lanka on public roads unless the listing says otherwise. Mileage limits still apply.

**Q: The car's AC stopped working. What do I do?**
A: Report it from **My Bookings → Report a problem**. The provider will arrange a fix or a replacement. If it can't be fixed, you may get a partial refund for the affected days.

**Q: Can I bring my pet?**
A: Only if the listing is marked **Pet friendly** or the provider agrees in writing through messages. A cleaning fee may apply.

## Providers and drivers

**Q: How much can I earn by listing my car?**
A: It depends on the vehicle, location and how often it is booked. You set your own daily rate. RideMachan keeps 12% (individual providers) or 10% (rental companies).

**Q: My car is 17 years old. Can I list it?**
A: No. Cars, vans and SUVs must be 15 years old or newer. Motorbikes and three-wheelers must be 10 years old or newer.

**Q: Do I need commercial insurance to list my car?**
A: Yes. The vehicle must have comprehensive insurance that allows rental use. Standard private insurance usually does not cover rentals. Check with your insurer.

**Q: Can I drive my own car for with-driver bookings?**
A: Yes, individual providers can act as the driver for their own vehicle if they also complete driver verification.

**Q: When do I get paid?**
A: Payouts are sent every Monday and Thursday, for rentals completed at least 48 hours earlier with no open dispute.

## General

**Q: Is RideMachan available as a mobile app?**
A: Yes, on Android and iOS. The website works on all devices too.

**Q: Which languages does RideMachan support?**
A: The app and support are available in English, Sinhala and Tamil.

**Q: How do I leave a review?**
A: After a rental ends, you get a notification to rate the vehicle, provider and driver (if any). You have 14 days to leave a review. Reviews cannot be edited after 48 hours.
