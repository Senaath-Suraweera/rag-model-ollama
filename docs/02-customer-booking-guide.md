# Customer Booking Guide

## How do I create a customer account?

1. Open the RideMachan app or website and select **Sign Up**.
2. Choose **Customer** as the account type.
3. Enter your full name, email address and mobile number.
4. Verify your mobile number with the one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS.
5. Set a password (at least 8 characters, with at least one number and one letter).
6. Complete your profile by uploading your identity documents (see below).

You can browse vehicles without an account, but you must have a verified account to make a booking.

## What documents do I need to rent a vehicle?

**For self-drive rentals:**
- A valid Sri Lankan National Identity Card (NIC) **or** a passport (for foreign nationals).
- A valid Sri Lankan driving licence for the vehicle class you are renting.
- Foreign nationals must have either a Sri Lankan temporary driving permit issued by the Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) / Automobile Association of Ceylon, or an International Driving Permit (IDP) endorsed for use in Sri Lanka.

**For with-driver rentals:**
- Only a valid NIC or passport is required. You do not need a driving licence.

Documents are reviewed by the RideMachan verification team, usually within 2 hours during support hours. You will get a notification when your account is verified.

## What are the age requirements?

- Self-drive cars, vans and SUVs: minimum age 21, with a licence held for at least 1 year.
- Luxury vehicles: minimum age 25, with a licence held for at least 3 years.
- Motorbikes and scooters: minimum age 18, with a valid motorcycle licence.
- With-driver bookings: the person making the booking must be at least 18.

## How do I book a vehicle?

1. Enter your **pick-up location**, **pick-up date and time** and **return date and time**.
2. Choose **Self-drive** or **With driver**.
3. Use filters (category, price, transmission, seats, fuel type, provider rating) to narrow results.
4. Open a vehicle listing to view photos, features, mileage allowance, deposit amount and reviews.
5. Select **Book Now** and review the price breakdown.
6. Add optional extras (child seat, extra driver, Wi-Fi device, airport delivery).
7. Pay online to confirm the booking.

Some providers use **Instant Book**, which confirms the booking immediately. Other providers use **Request to Book**: the provider must accept within 4 hours. If they do not respond in time, the request expires and you are not charged.

## How long can I rent a vehicle for?

- Minimum rental: 1 day (24 hours) for cars, vans and SUVs; 6 hours for motorbikes and three-wheelers.
- Maximum single booking: 30 days. For longer rentals, contact the provider through in-app messages to arrange a long-term agreement, or extend your booking before it ends.

## How do I extend my booking?

Go to **My Bookings → Active booking → Request Extension**, choose the new return date and time, and submit. The provider has 2 hours to accept. If accepted, the extra cost is charged to your saved payment method. If the vehicle is already booked by someone else, the extension cannot be approved.

Returning a vehicle late without an approved extension results in late return charges (see *Pricing, Payments and Deposits*).

## Pick-up process

1. Arrive at the agreed location at the booked time and show your NIC/passport and driving licence.
2. Complete the **digital vehicle inspection** in the app together with the provider: take photos of all four sides, the interior, the dashboard (fuel level and odometer) and any existing damage.
3. Both you and the provider confirm the inspection in the app. The rental officially starts at this moment.

Always complete the pick-up inspection. Damage that is not recorded at pick-up may be treated as new damage at return.

## Return process

1. Return the vehicle to the agreed location by the return time, with the same fuel level as at pick-up.
2. Complete the return inspection in the app with the provider (same photos as pick-up).
3. The provider confirms the return. If there is no new damage, the security deposit release begins automatically.

## Can someone else drive the car I rented?

Only drivers named on the booking may drive a self-drive vehicle. You can add an **extra driver** during booking or from **My Bookings** before pick-up. Every extra driver must upload their own licence and be verified. Letting an unlisted person drive cancels your insurance cover for that trip.

## Can I take the vehicle anywhere in Sri Lanka?

Yes, vehicles may be driven anywhere in Sri Lanka on public roads, unless the listing says otherwise. The following are not allowed:
- Off-road driving (unless the listing is an off-road-approved 4x4).
- Racing, driving lessons, towing, or using the vehicle for paid ride-hailing.
- Taking the vehicle on a ferry without the provider's written approval in in-app messages.
- Smoking or carrying pets without the provider's permission.
