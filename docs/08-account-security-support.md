# Accounts, Security, Notifications and Support

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

1. On the login page, select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address or mobile number on your account.
3. Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to you. The OTP is valid for **5 minutes**.
4. Set a new password.

If you do not receive the OTP, wait 60 seconds and select **Resend OTP**. You can request up to 5 OTPs per hour.

## How do I change my password?

When logged in, go to **Profile → Security → Change Password**. Enter your current password and the new password twice. The new password must be at least 8 characters with at least one letter and one number, and must be different from your last 3 passwords. You will be logged out of all other devices.

## How do I change my email or mobile number?

Go to **Profile → Personal details**, edit the email or mobile number and verify the new one with the OTP sent to it. For security, changing your mobile number also sends a notification to your old email address.

## Why was my account locked?

Your account is temporarily locked for **15 minutes** after 5 failed login attempts in a row. You can wait or reset your password to unlock it right away.

## Can I have more than one role (for example, customer and provider)?

Yes. You can use the same login for a customer account and a provider or driver account. Switch between them from **Profile → Switch role**. Each role is verified separately.

## How do I update my profile picture or details?

Go to **Profile → Edit**. You can change your profile photo, name (requires re-verification if it no longer matches your ID), address and languages. Changes to identity documents must be approved by the verification team.

## Why is my account "Pending verification"?

New accounts, new documents and changed identity details must be checked by the RideMachan verification team. You can browse while pending, but you cannot book, list vehicles or accept trips. If verification takes longer than stated, contact support.

## Why was my account suspended?

Accounts can be suspended for:
- Fake or expired documents
- Repeated cancellations or no-shows
- Low ratings over a long period
- Payments or bookings arranged outside RideMachan
- Unsafe driving, harassment, discrimination or fraud
- Unpaid charges

You will get an email explaining the reason. You can appeal within 14 days from **Help → Appeal suspension**. An administrator reviews the appeal within 5 working days.

## How do I delete my account?

Go to **Profile → Settings → Delete account**. You cannot delete an account with active bookings, open disputes, unpaid charges or pending payouts. Once deleted, your personal data is removed within 30 days, except for records we must keep by law (such as payment and tax records, kept for 7 years).

## Notifications

You receive notifications for:
- Booking requests, confirmations, changes and cancellations
- Pick-up and return reminders (24 hours and 1 hour before)
- Payments, refunds, deposits and payouts
- New messages
- Document expiry warnings (providers and drivers)
- Account and security alerts

Manage which notifications you get by push, email and SMS in **Profile → Notification settings**. Security alerts and booking confirmations cannot be turned off.

## Messages

Use **Messages** to talk to the other people in your booking. Messages are linked to the booking and can be reviewed by RideMachan support if there is a dispute. Do not share personal bank details or arrange payments through messages. Phone numbers are shared only 24 hours before pick-up.

## Privacy and data

RideMachan collects only the data needed to run rentals: identity and licence details, contact details, payment information (handled by our PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway – RideMachan does not store full card numbers), booking history and, during active trips, vehicle location. Location tracking stops when a trip ends. We follow the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022. You can request a copy of your data from **Profile → Privacy → Download my data**.

## How do I contact support?

| Channel | Availability | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| In-app chat (**Help → Chat with us**) | 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily | Most questions, bookings, payments |
| Hotline | 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily | Urgent booking issues |
| Emergency line / in-app Emergency button | 24/7 | Accidents, breakdowns, safety issues |
| Email support | Reply within 24 hours | Documents, disputes, appeals |
| Help Centre | 24/7 | Self-service guides and FAQs |

Support is available in **Sinhala, Tamil and English**. Have your booking ID ready (it starts with **RM-**, for example RM-2026-004512).

## Reporting a user or listing

To report a provider, driver, customer or listing for misconduct, fraud or safety concerns, open their profile or the booking and select **Report**. Reports are reviewed by administrators within 24 hours. Safety reports are handled first.
