# Insurance, Damage, Breakdowns and Accidents

## Is my rental insured?

Yes. Every vehicle on RideMachan must have valid **comprehensive insurance** that allows rental use. This covers third-party liability and damage to the rented vehicle, subject to the **excess** (the amount you pay before insurance covers the rest).

Insurance is only valid if:
- The booking was made and paid through RideMachan.
- The vehicle was driven by a driver named on the booking (or the assigned RideMachan driver).
- The driver was not under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- The vehicle was used in line with the rental rules (no racing, off-road use, towing, etc.).
- Any accident was reported to the police and to RideMachan as described below.

## What is the excess (deductible)?

| Category | Standard excess | With Damage Protection Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Motorbike / three-wheeler | LKR 15,000 | LKR 5,000 |
| Economy car / sedan | LKR 40,000 | LKR 10,000 |
| SUV / van | LKR 75,000 | LKR 20,000 |
| Luxury | LKR 150,000 | LKR 40,000 |

The excess is the maximum you pay for damage to the vehicle in a covered incident. It does not apply to damage caused by breaking the rental rules – in that case, you may have to pay the full cost.

## What is Damage Protection Plus?

Damage Protection Plus is an optional add-on that reduces your excess (see table above). It costs **LKR 1,200 per day** for cars, vans and SUVs, **LKR 2,500 per day** for luxury vehicles, and **LKR 400 per day** for motorbikes and three-wheelers. It must be added at booking or before pick-up. It cannot be added after the rental starts.

Damage Protection Plus does **not** cover: tyres and windscreens (unless in an accident), lost keys, interior damage from spills or smoking, fuel-related engine damage (wrong fuel), or damage while breaking the rental rules.

## What should I do if I have an accident?

1. **Stop safely and make sure everyone is safe.** Call **1990** (Suwa Seriya ambulance) if anyone is injured.
2. **Call the police (119)** and get a police report. In Sri Lanka, a police report is required for insurance claims. Do not move the vehicle until the police arrive, unless it is a danger to traffic.
3. **Call the RideMachan 24/7 emergency line** or tap **Emergency** in the active booking.
4. Take photos of all vehicles, number plates, the scene and any damage. Get the other driver's name, phone number, licence and insurance details.
5. Do not admit fault or make private settlements with the other party.
6. Upload photos and the police report in the app under **My Bookings → Report incident** within **24 hours**.

The provider will also notify their insurance company. RideMachan supports both sides through the claim.

## What if the vehicle breaks down?

1. Move to a safe place and switch on hazard lights.
2. Tap **Emergency → Breakdown** in the active booking, or call the 24/7 line.
3. RideMachan contacts the provider, who arranges roadside assistance or a replacement vehicle.

If the breakdown is caused by a mechanical fault (not misuse), you are not charged for repairs, and the unused rental time is refunded or a replacement vehicle is provided. Breakdowns caused by misuse – for example, using the wrong fuel or driving through deep floodwater – are charged to the customer.

## Flat tyres

Most vehicles carry a spare tyre and tools. You may change it yourself if it is safe, or request roadside help. Tyre damage that is not caused by an accident is charged at the actual repair or replacement cost, unless it was caused by a defect.

## Damage found at return

If the return inspection shows new damage that was not in the pick-up inspection:

1. The provider records the damage with photos in the return inspection.
2. The provider submits a repair estimate or invoice within **5 days**.
3. You are notified and can accept or dispute it within 7 days.
4. Accepted charges are deducted from your deposit, up to your excess limit.

Small scratches under 2 cm and stone chips that do not crack the glass are treated as normal wear and are not charged.

## Theft

If the vehicle is stolen, report it to the police immediately and contact the RideMachan emergency line. You must return all keys. If the keys were left in the vehicle or the vehicle was left unlocked, insurance may not cover the theft and you may be responsible for the full value.

## Traffic fines

Traffic fines and parking fines during your rental are your responsibility. In Sri Lanka, spot fines are usually paid at a post office. If a fine is sent to the provider after your rental, it is charged to you plus a LKR 500 admin fee.

## Safety features in the app

- **Live trip tracking** for active bookings, visible to the provider and RideMachan support.
- **Share trip** – send your live location to a family member or friend.
- **Emergency button** – connects you to RideMachan's 24/7 line and shares your location.
- Verified profiles – all providers and drivers have their documents checked by RideMachan.
