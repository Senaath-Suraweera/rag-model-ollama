# Cancellation and Refund Policy

## How do I cancel a booking?

Go to **My Bookings**, open the booking and select **Cancel Booking**. Choose a reason and confirm. The refund amount is shown before you confirm. You will get a cancellation confirmation by notification and email.

## Refunds when the customer cancels

The refund depends on how long before the pick-up time you cancel:

| When you cancel | Refund of rental amount |
|---|---|
| Within 1 hour of booking (and more than 24 hours before pick-up) | 100% (free cancellation window) |
| More than 72 hours before pick-up | 100% |
| 24 – 72 hours before pick-up | 50% |
| Less than 24 hours before pick-up | 0% (one day's rental charged; remaining days refunded for bookings of 3+ days) |
| No-show (customer does not arrive within 2 hours of pick-up time) | 0% |

The **RideMachan service fee is refunded only** when the refund is 100%.

## Refunds when the provider or driver cancels

If a provider, rental company or driver cancels a confirmed booking:

- You receive a **100% refund**, including the service fee.
- RideMachan will try to find you a similar or better vehicle at no extra cost. If the replacement costs more, RideMachan covers the difference for up to 3 days.
- If the cancellation is less than 24 hours before pick-up, you also receive a LKR 2,000 RideMachan credit.

Providers who cancel confirmed bookings are penalised (see *Vehicle Provider Guide*).

## Request to Book that is declined or expires

If the provider declines your request or does not respond within 4 hours, the authorisation on your card is released. You are not charged anything.

## Cancelling after pick-up (ending a rental early)

If you return the vehicle earlier than booked, unused full days are refunded at 50% of the daily rate. Weekly or monthly discounts are recalculated based on the actual rental length.

## Cancellation due to emergencies

For documented emergencies – serious illness or injury, death in the immediate family, natural disaster, government curfew, or a flight cancelled by the airline – contact support with proof within 7 days. Approved cases receive a full refund regardless of timing.

## Vehicle problems at pick-up

If the vehicle is unsafe, very different from the listing, or not available at the agreed time, do not accept it. Report it in the app under **My Bookings → Report a problem** before starting the rental. You will receive a full refund or a replacement vehicle.

## How long do refunds take?

- Refunds are processed by RideMachan within **2 working days**.
- Card refunds take a further **5–10 working days** to reach your account, depending on your bank.
- Mobile wallet refunds usually arrive within 24 hours.
- RideMachan credit is added instantly and can be used on any future booking within 12 months.

## Disputing a charge

If you do not agree with a charge (damage, fuel, cleaning, late return), open a dispute from **My Bookings → Receipt → Dispute charge** within **7 days** of the charge. Include photos or other evidence. The provider has 48 hours to respond, and a RideMachan administrator makes a final decision within 5 working days. The pick-up and return inspection photos are the main evidence used.
