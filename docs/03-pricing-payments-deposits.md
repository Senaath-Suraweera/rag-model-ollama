# Pricing, Payments and Deposits

All prices on RideMachan are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) and include VAT where applicable.

## How is the rental price calculated?

The total price shown at checkout is made up of:

1. **Daily (or hourly) rate** set by the provider × number of rental days.
2. **Driver fee** (with-driver bookings only).
3. **Extras** you selected (child seat, extra driver, airport delivery, etc.).
4. **RideMachan service fee** – 8% of the rental subtotal, charged to the customer.
5. **Security deposit** – a refundable hold, shown separately (see below).

A rental "day" is a 24-hour period from the pick-up time. Returning up to 59 minutes late is free; after that, late return charges apply.

## Typical daily rates

These are guide prices; each provider sets their own rate.

| Category | Self-drive (per day) | With driver (per day, 8 hrs / 100 km) |
|---|---|---|
| Economy car | LKR 6,500 – 9,000 | LKR 11,000 – 14,000 |
| Sedan | LKR 9,000 – 13,000 | LKR 14,000 – 18,000 |
| SUV | LKR 15,000 – 30,000 | LKR 20,000 – 38,000 |
| Van | LKR 14,000 – 20,000 | LKR 18,000 – 26,000 |
| Motorbike / scooter | LKR 2,500 – 4,500 | Not available |
| Three-wheeler | LKR 3,500 – 5,000 | LKR 6,000 – 8,000 |
| Luxury | LKR 35,000+ | LKR 45,000+ |

Weekly bookings (7+ days) often get a discount of 10–15%, and monthly bookings (28+ days) 20–30%, depending on the provider.

## Mileage allowance

- Self-drive: most listings include **150 km per day**. Extra kilometres are charged at the rate shown on the listing, typically LKR 40–80 per km depending on category. Some listings offer unlimited mileage.
- With driver: the daily rate includes **8 hours and 100 km**. Extra hours are charged at LKR 500–800 per hour and extra kilometres at LKR 50–90 per km.

## Fuel policy

RideMachan uses a **same-to-same** fuel policy. Return the vehicle with the same fuel level recorded at pick-up. If the level is lower, you are charged for the missing fuel at the current pump price plus a LKR 1,000 refuelling fee. For with-driver bookings, fuel is either included or charged separately, as stated on the listing.

## Driver accommodation and meals

For multi-day with-driver trips outside the driver's home district, the customer must provide or pay for the driver's accommodation and meals. If you do not arrange this, a driver allowance of LKR 2,500 per night is added to the booking.

## Which payment methods are accepted?

- Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit/debit cards
- Local bank debit cards supported by our payment gateway
- Bank transfer (for bookings made at least 48 hours in advance; booking is confirmed once payment is received)
- Mobile wallets: FriMi, Genie and eZ Cash

Cash payments to providers or drivers are **not allowed** for rental charges. Bookings paid outside RideMachan are not covered by insurance, refunds or support.

## When am I charged?

- **Instant Book:** charged in full when you book.
- **Request to Book:** your card is authorised when you send the request and charged only when the provider accepts.
- **Extras added later and extensions:** charged when approved.
- **Additional charges after return** (fuel, extra km, late return, cleaning, damage): charged to your saved payment method within 72 hours of return, with an itemised receipt.

## Security deposit

A refundable security deposit is held on your card at pick-up time. Typical amounts:

| Category | Deposit |
|---|---|
| Motorbike / three-wheeler | LKR 10,000 |
| Economy car / sedan | LKR 25,000 |
| SUV / van | LKR 50,000 |
| Luxury | LKR 100,000 |

With-driver bookings do not require a security deposit.

The deposit is a **hold**, not a charge. It is released within **3 working days** after a return with no issues. Depending on your bank, the money may take 5–10 working days to appear back in your account. If there are additional charges, they are deducted from the deposit and the remainder is released.

## Late return charges

- Up to 59 minutes late: no charge.
- 1–3 hours late: 25% of the daily rate per hour started, up to one full day.
- More than 3 hours late: one full extra day, plus any loss the next customer suffers if their booking cannot go ahead.

## Other fees

| Fee | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cleaning fee (excessive dirt, sand, food or smoke smell) | LKR 3,000 – 7,500 |
| Lost key | Actual replacement cost, minimum LKR 15,000 |
| Traffic fines and parking fines | Actual amount + LKR 500 admin fee |
| Airport delivery / collection | LKR 3,000 – 6,000 per trip |
| Child seat | LKR 500 per day |
| Extra driver | LKR 750 per day |

## How do providers get paid?

Providers receive the rental amount minus the **RideMachan commission** (12% for individual providers, 10% for rental companies) 48 hours after the vehicle is returned with no open disputes. Payouts go to the bank account saved in the provider dashboard under **Earnings → Payout settings**. Payouts are processed every Monday and Thursday.
