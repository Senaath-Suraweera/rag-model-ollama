# About RideMachan

## What is RideMachan?

RideMachan is a vehicle rental marketplace for Sri Lanka. It connects people who need a vehicle with people and businesses who have vehicles to rent. Customers can rent a vehicle to drive themselves (self-drive) or rent a vehicle together with a driver (chauffeur-driven). All bookings, payments, messages and support requests are handled inside the RideMachan platform.

The name comes from the Sri Lankan word "machan", a friendly way of saying "buddy" or "mate". RideMachan aims to make renting a vehicle feel as easy as borrowing one from a friend, while still being safe, insured and fully tracked.

## Who uses RideMachan?

RideMachan has five types of users:

- **Customers** – people who search for, book and pay for vehicles.
- **Individual vehicle providers** – private owners who list their own vehicle(s) for rent.
- **Rental companies** – registered businesses that list and manage a fleet of vehicles.
- **Drivers** – verified drivers who can be hired with a vehicle. A driver can work independently or be attached to a rental company.
- **Maintenance staff** – staff who inspect, service and repair vehicles, usually on behalf of rental companies.

The platform is run by **RideMachan administrators**, who verify users and vehicles, resolve disputes, review reports and manage the overall platform.

| Economy car | Suzuki Alto, Perodua Axia, Suzuki Wagon R | 4 |
| Sedan | Toyota Axio, Honda Grace, Toyota Premio | 5 |
| SUV | Toyota Land Cruiser Prado, Mitsubishi Montero, Honda Vezel | 5–7 |
| Van | Toyota KDH, Nissan Caravan | 9–14 |
| Motorbike / scooter | Honda Dio, Yamaha FZ, Bajaj Pulsar | 1–2 |
| Three-wheeler (tuk-tuk) | Bajaj RE | 2–3 |

The exact vehicles available depend on location and dates. Every listing shows photos, seat count, transmission (auto/manual), fuel type, mileage policy and the provider's rating.

## Where does RideMachan operate?

RideMachan currently serves all 25 districts of Sri Lanka, with the most vehicles available in Colombo, Gampaha, Kandy, Galle, Negombo and Jaffna. Airport pick-up is available at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB, Katunayake) and Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport (HRI) from selected providers.

## Operating hours

- The RideMachan app and website are available 24/7.
- Customer support chat and hotline: 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Sri Lanka time, UTC+5:30), every day including public holidays.
- Emergency roadside and accident line: 24/7.

## Key features

- Search and filter vehicles by location, dates, category, price, transmission and seats.
- Self-drive or with-driver bookings.
- Secure online payments and refundable security deposits.
- Real-time trip tracking for active rentals.
- In-app messaging between customers, providers and drivers.
- Ratings and reviews after every completed rental.
- Notifications for booking confirmations, reminders, payment updates and messages.
- Dashboards for providers, rental companies, drivers and administrators.
