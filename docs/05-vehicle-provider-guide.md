# Vehicle Provider Guide

This guide is for **individual vehicle providers** (private owners) and **rental companies** (registered businesses) who list vehicles on RideMachan.

## What is the difference between an individual provider and a rental company?

| | Individual provider | Rental company |
|---|---|---|
| Who | A private person renting out their own vehicle(s) | A registered business with a fleet |
| Vehicle limit | Up to 3 vehicles | Unlimited |
| Business registration | Not required | Business Registration (BR) certificate required |
| Drivers | Can drive their own vehicle for with-driver bookings | Can add and manage their own drivers |
| Maintenance staff | Not available | Can add maintenance staff accounts |
| Commission | 12% | 10% |

An individual provider who wants to list more than 3 vehicles must upgrade to a rental company account.

## How do I register as an individual provider?

1. Sign up and choose **Vehicle Provider → Individual**.
2. Verify your mobile number with OTP.
3. Upload your NIC (front and back) and a recent utility bill as proof of address.
4. Add your bank account details for payouts.
5. Add your first vehicle (see *Listing a vehicle*).

Verification usually takes 1–2 working days.

## How does a rental company register?

1. Sign up and choose **Vehicle Provider → Rental Company**.
2. Enter the company name, BR number, registered address and contact person.
3. Upload the BR certificate and the NIC of the authorised person.
4. Add a company bank account for payouts.
5. Submit the request. A RideMachan administrator reviews it, and may call the company to confirm details.

Approval usually takes 2–3 working days. The request status is shown in the dashboard as **Pending**, **Approved** or **Rejected** (with a reason).

## Can an individual provider join a rental company?

Yes. An individual provider can send a request to a rental company from **Rental Companies → Request to join**. If the company accepts, the provider's vehicles are managed under the company and the company's commission rate applies. The provider can leave at any time once there are no active bookings.

## Listing a vehicle

To add a vehicle, go to **Vehicle Fleet → Add Vehicle** and provide:

- Make, model, year, colour, registration number and chassis number
- Category, transmission, fuel type, seat count
- At least 6 clear photos (front, back, both sides, interior front, interior back)
- Vehicle registration certificate (CR book)
- Valid revenue licence
- Valid **comprehensive insurance** that allows commercial rental use
- Valid emission test certificate
- Daily rate, mileage allowance, extra-km rate and deposit amount
- Availability calendar and pick-up locations
- Booking mode: **Instant Book** or **Request to Book**

Vehicle requirements:
- Cars, vans and SUVs must be **15 years old or newer**. Motorbikes and three-wheelers must be 10 years old or newer.
- The vehicle must be roadworthy, clean and have working air conditioning (for cars, vans and SUVs).

Each vehicle is reviewed by an administrator before it appears in search results.

## Keeping documents up to date

The dashboard warns you 30 days before a revenue licence, insurance or emission certificate expires. If a document expires, the vehicle is **automatically hidden** from search and cannot receive new bookings until a valid document is uploaded and approved.

## Managing bookings

- **Request to Book:** accept or decline within 4 hours. Repeated non-responses lower your listing's ranking.
- **Instant Book:** keep your availability calendar accurate so you are not booked for days you cannot provide the vehicle.
- Always complete the **pick-up and return inspections** in the app. These photos are your main evidence if there is damage.

## Assigning and unassigning vehicles (rental companies)

Rental companies can assign a vehicle to a specific driver or maintenance staff member from **Vehicle Fleet → Vehicle → Assign**. To unassign, open the vehicle and select **Unassign**. A vehicle cannot be unassigned from a driver while it is on an active trip.

## Maintenance mode

When a vehicle needs servicing or repairs, set it to **Maintenance** in the fleet page. It is hidden from search for the selected period. Rental companies can create a maintenance task and assign it to maintenance staff, who update its status (**Pending → In Progress → Completed**) and upload service records.

## Provider cancellation penalties

Cancelling a confirmed booking harms customers. Penalties:

- First cancellation in 90 days: warning, and the calendar is blocked for the cancelled dates.
- Second cancellation in 90 days: LKR 2,500 fee deducted from the next payout.
- Three or more in 90 days: listing suspended for review by an administrator.

Cancellations caused by documented emergencies or vehicle breakdowns reported with proof are not penalised.

## Ratings and ranking

Customers rate the vehicle and provider from 1 to 5 stars after each rental. Search ranking depends on rating, response time, acceptance rate, cancellation rate and price. Providers with an average rating below 3.5 over their last 10 rentals are reviewed by an administrator and may be suspended.

## Payouts

See *Pricing, Payments and Deposits → How do providers get paid?* Payouts are sent every Monday and Thursday, 48 hours after a completed rental with no open dispute. Earnings reports can be downloaded from **Earnings → Reports** as CSV or PDF.
